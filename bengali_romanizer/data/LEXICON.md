# Bengali pronunciation lexicon

`lexicon.tsv.gz` is a compressed, otherwise unmodified copy of Google's
[Bengali pronunciation dictionary](https://github.com/google/language-resources/blob/master/bn/data/lexicon.tsv).
It was transcribed by native-speaker experts for standard Bangladeshi Bengali
and is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
